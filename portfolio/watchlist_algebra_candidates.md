## Track Summary

The Algebra Candidates track is the most diverse track in the corpus. It covers five distinct sub-theories that share algebraic and dynamical structure:

1. **8fold / GECT (Geometric Entropy and Complexity Theory)** — The largest sub-theory. Covers the A9/A7 dynamical algebra, GECT unified framework, crystal taxonomy, lambda functions, and applications to P vs NP, NP-completeness, Goldbach, and Yang-Mills mass gap.
2. **13-16 Theorem / Phi Law** — A conjectured scaling law and squarefree frame rigidity result, with phi-residue large sieve inequality. Includes multiple paper drafts and LaTeX sources.
3. **MCE (Market Coherence Energy) Engine** — Applied algebraic physics framework; overlaps with Cross_Domain_Methods. Multiple specification revisions and session reports.
4. **P vs NP / Puzzle challenges** — Geometric resolution via A9 dynamical algebra; BTC puzzle 71/135 factorization work.
5. **Goldbach / ABC / Yang-Mills** — Standalone proof drafts for major open problems.

**⚠ Caution:** Multiple sub-theories in this track make very high-impact claims (P≠NP, Yang-Mills mass gap, Goldbach). Per swarm governance, these require the full 9-dimension rubric + two-model acceptance quorum before elevation above `C-Underspecified`. The Refuter Agent should be assigned immediately after Catalog Agent seeding.
