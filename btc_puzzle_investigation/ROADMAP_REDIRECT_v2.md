## 0. The two governing principles (read first)

**(I) Mission / counter-prior** (`CLAUDE.md`, `feedback_suppression_bias_asymmetric_2026_08_02`).
secp — the j=0/μ₆ curve and the keys/sets it produces — is **assumed to carry exploitable structure**,
and the task is to find *how* it leaks. The training default ("secure, nothing here, generic/wall") is
a reflex to suspend, not evidence. **Bias is one-way:** we suppress finds, we do not inflate them — so
every lead is **surfaced and tested**, never pre-deflated. The safeguard against a false find is the
*test* (matched null / firing control / OOS / exact arithmetic), not pre-test skepticism. **Burden of
proof is on the closure:** to call a channel dead/generic/bounded-to-nothing you must name the object,
the class of observable it closes, and its power and scope — the same bar as a `[PROVED]`.

**(II) Bricks, not a hole-in-one.** No item is scored on whether it "opens the gate." The unit of
progress is a **brick**: a result that (1) **stands alone** — verifiable from a named artifact; (2)
**is reusable** — an exact law, a channel bounded *with power and scope*, a dissolved anomaly restated
as a sharper law, or a verified citation; (3) **is true in the direction it claims** — carries its
matched null / control / OOS / grade, so it never has to be un-built. A brick that **bounds** a channel
counts *iff* it carries power + scope. A dismissal-by-declaration is a fake brick; it crumbles when
leaned on. This is the Anomaly-Drill rule as a construction principle: **a drill ends in a sharper law,
a bounded magnitude with its power, or a named next probe — never a "door shut."** Dissolution is a
positive result.

**The mortar (non-negotiable on every empirical brick).** Exact arithmetic (`int`/`Fraction`, never
float in the signal path); a **matched null** sharing the readout's forced symmetries (a value-
permutation null against an even-in-`d` readout is a *guaranteed* false positive — fired twice); a
**firing control** that must register a planted effect; **out-of-sample** retest before naming a zero;
**count only unforced hits**; `experiment-designer` **pre-registration mandatory before data**. Every
output carries a **grade** and, if empirical, its **scale** — the banner (p ≤ 10⁵ measured vs secp's
2²⁵⁶, ~239 binary orders) governs every measured claim.

## 1. The bricks (ordered by dependency, not difficulty)

Each brick lists the **object**, the **brick laid in *either* outcome**, the **method/mortar**, the
**grade target**, and the **not-a-hole-in-one** clause.

### Brick A — E3 cubic difference-set: discharge the owed measurement
- **Object.** E3 group-law cubic difference-set energy on the real 82 keys — a *fired pre-registered
  reject* (`E3=13125`, 98.4th pctile, p≈0.033 **uncorrected**, interpretation OPEN), on the one
  difference-set class that touches the DLP. Master v12 flags it **A-1d ★★** as the standout suppressed
  positive that "owes a MEASUREMENT," deflated to a null at a consolidation seam.
- **Brick either way.** *Leak:* a reproduced, MC-corrected, control-clean effect on a DLP-touching
  object = the most consequential structural brick in the corpus. *Null:* converts the standout owed
  positive into a **bounded magnitude with power + scope**, retiring a hanging seam honestly. Only a
  *re-deflation without a test* would be a loss.
- **Method / mortar.** Pre-register with `experiment-designer`. **Independent-arm reproduction** — vary
  the band-random arms, not just the null pool (the exact flaw that faked the χ₂ cousin,
  `RESULT_chi2_diffset_FALSE_POSITIVE_v1.md`). MC-correction across the pre-registered family. Control
  curve (P-256). Firing control (plant an E2/E3 effect, confirm it registers). Exact arithmetic; OOS
  arms.
- **Grade target.** `[VERIFIED/EMPIRICAL @ tested scale]` or `[DISSOLVED-ON-TEST]`, each with power +
  scope. Not a security claim.
- **Not a hole-in-one.** Even a fired reproduction is a scoped structural fact at p ≤ 10⁵ under the
  banner — a brick, not a keyspace reduction.

### Brick B — the public-ε linchpin (resolve the arsenal's single dependency)
- **Object.** Is there a **public** route to the lift carry ε via the Eisenstein-lift norm `eis(X)`
  (`…MASTER_v12.md` §D / doc 19 §4A)? The whole conditional d-shadow arsenal is gated on this one input;
  currently **circular** and **untested**. Sub-item N16 owes the declared-not-tested cross-check
  `|u−v|≤½ == build.eps` on the project script.
- **Brick either way.** *Yes (public):* un-gates the amplifiers on covered points and makes N16's
  flap==carry question concrete. *No:* collapses a circular arsenal cleanly and confirms the flap≠carry
  gap (N16) at the object level — a durable brick that redirects the flap program. Either resolves the
  **single most load-bearing dependency** in the map.
- **Method / mortar.** First an **analytical** brick: is `eis(X)` computable from public `(x,y)` with
  no DLP-cost object, poly-time, single-point? Build it; then run the small, decisive N16 cross-check
  (`|u−v|≤½ == build.eps`). Exact arithmetic throughout.
- **Grade target.** `[PROVED]` that `eis` is / is not public, and a graded N16 object-identity result.
- **Not a hole-in-one.** A public ε makes the *conditional* amplifiers non-circular on covered points —
  still under the banner; not a break.

### Brick C — single-point cheap partial of the 0.93 (x,y) shadow  *(sharpened in v2 by idea 2)*
- **Object.** The joint `(x,y)` readout reaches `ρ_inf = 0.93–0.96` (M14) vs x-only 0.29–0.36; the gap
  is parity — x-only is **even in `d`** (`U(−a,b,c)=U(a,b,−c)`, even-subspace ceiling `1/√2`), the
  y/`xy` coordinate supplies the **odd, μ₃-weight-1** half. The N1 **2+2 splitting** (only 2 of 4
  eigenvalues carry weight-1 mass at the maximizing frequency) is the exact structure to target.
- **★ The guardrail that reframes the goal (M14 / v6).** The 0.93 is a **forced magnitude, not a
  leak**: the l²-scalar-fidelity statistic that produces it is *machine-identical* (`Σ|U|²=p²(n−1)`) for
  the real character and for a random function of `d` — "the control validates nothing about the lead."
  So the objective is **not** "capture the 0.93" (non-diagnostic); it is **find any statistic/basis
  that separates character from random at all.** Already bounded on the covered slices: M19/M20 show no
  concentration above `φ` for the μ₆-isotype-pure and coordinate-bit readouts.
- **The untested slice = the brick.** A **non-μ₆-covariant, point-specific** readout — an **oblique
  (non-unitary) basis** `M` on the single eigenspace of the 2+2 splitting, computable from one public
  `(x,y)`, and **multi-point** joint readouts — tested for separation against a **symmetry-matched**
  null. *Leak:* first statistic that separates → directly consequential. *Null:* a matched-null bound
  on the single-eigenspace / oblique channel at stated power → the honest residue of §XI.1, a brick.
- **Method / mortar.** `experiment-designer` pre-reg. Target **disjoint** from the readout (no
  residue-vs-component tautology, §XII.24). Null **symmetry-matched** (even-in-`d` guard — the M14
  control is *forced* and must not be reused as the null). Firing control (plant a separating readout,
  confirm it registers *above* the forced l² floor). Exact arithmetic; OOS.
- **Grade target.** Characterization + bound, with power + scope.
- **Not a hole-in-one.** A positive separation is a bit-rate at tested scale under the banner, not a
  group-ceiling break.

### Brick E — D2 / A_new non-homomorphic covariant (does `ℓ(d)` carry any `d`-bit?)  *(new in v2, idea 1)*
- **Object.** N14: the isotypic trace `ℓ(d)=dlog_{A_new}(S([d]G))`. Correct arithmetic behind it:
  `Hom(E,A_new)=0` (P13, the homomorphic wall), and `A_new`'s **internal DLP is easy** — MOV embedding
  degree **k=3** (M17). `ℓ` is orbit-covariant (`ℓ(λd)=k·ℓ(d)`) but **not** affine/homomorphic in `d`.
- **Caveats the source drops (attach them).** (a) `A_new` supersingular *at secp* is **N2** — a
  dimension-2 extrapolation, `[PROVED §A1.1]` but with owed independent re-derivation of its two
  external inputs. (b) The xedni obstruction it proposes to "bypass" is itself `[HEURISTIC]`. (c) The
  specific *mod-6 keyspace-reduction use* of the D2 bridge is a **closed null** (N17, net 0 bits) — the
  brick below is **not** that use.
- **Brick either way.** The honest, scoped question (scale-blocked at p=43, 15 points): does the
  non-homomorphic covariant `ℓ(d)` carry **any partial `d`-info** beyond the orbit label, against a
  firing control? *Yes:* names a new non-homomorphic channel to characterize (symmetry type / which
  eigenspace). *No, at power:* bounds the `ℓ` channel at p=43 — a brick, scoped to that prime.
- **Method / mortar.** `experiment-designer` pre-reg. Positive control must fire (plant an affine/
  quadratic-in-GLV-halves `d`-dependence and confirm `ℓ` detects it — this control already exists in
  `RESULT_isotypic_trace_lever_status_v1`). Exact arithmetic. Report as characterization, not a transfer
  map.
- **Grade target.** `[EMPIRICAL @ p=43]` bound or a named channel; **no** secp claim (N2 + scale).
- **Not a hole-in-one.** Even a positive `d`-bit at p=43 sits behind the (easy) `A_new` dlog and is
  scale-blocked; it is a channel to characterize, not "a poly-time invertible scalar mapping."

- **Not a hole-in-one.** These sharpen the map and protect the other bricks; none is itself a leakage

## 2. How to work it slowly (steady cadence, one brick at a time)

- **Dependency order, not difficulty order.** B's N16 cross-check is small and decisive for flap==carry
  → first, de-risks the flap program. A is the standout owed measurement and exercises the difference-
  set machinery C also needs → A before C. E is scoped to p=43 and shares the isotypic-trace harness
  → after B. **D runs in parallel throughout** as the scale-immune track (D3 gates any citation upgrade;
  D4 is small and pre-registerable now).
- **One brick, one loop:** pre-register (if empirical) → build → matched-null test → OOS → grade → fold
  the artifact into the ledgers with its power and scope. No brick promotes a grade without its named
  artifact in the same edit.
- **Trust-but-verify** every subagent artifact against the diff before it becomes a brick.

## 3. What we are explicitly NOT doing (guardrails, stated positively)

- **Not** chasing "collapse the search space" / "decidable isolation of `d`" framings.
- **Not** treating a **work-invariant** (P19), **forced-magnitude** (M14 0.93), or already-**bounded**
  channel as a lever; a revisit must add a channel beyond what the existing proof/bound covers.
- **Not** resurrecting the **Dirichlet-island keyspace-pruning** apparatus (N18 closed null) — only its
  narrow surviving correlation question (D4).
- **Not** reading any uncorrected p-value, any tested-scale (p ≤ 10⁵) result, or any dimension-2
  extrapolation (N2) as reaching secp — only algebraic bricks with no measured input (Track D, plus
  P1/P2/P8/P9–10/P15) do.
- **Not** the PRNG / generation side — a research-interest boundary (`CLAUDE.md` scope).
- **Not** converting a scoped null into a closure — burden of proof stays on the closure.
