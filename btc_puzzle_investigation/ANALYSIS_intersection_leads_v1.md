## INTERSECTION α ★★ — N7's deficit is the L2-budget COST of P18's proved cubic coherence  *(O1)*

**The two legs.** `[grounded]`
- **P18 (PROVED):** the real curve carries a large *coherent* cubic term `Σ_b χ₂(b)T(a₀,b)³ = i√p·(n²−O(n))`
  — a genuine, non-random concentration in the cubic phase, locked to `χ₂(b)` (M28, survives the parity-
  matched null, `|mean_QR|≈0.33` and widening).
- **N7 (OPEN):** the DLP character's mean harmonic magnitude runs **0.06–0.10% below a random unit-phase
  target's** (Z=−13.99, 7/7 primes, persistent over 79× `p`). Its *own* current mechanism candidate is the
  **row-budget conservation `Σ_a|U(a,b,c)|²=n(n−1)`** — a fixed L2 budget.

**The NEW hypothesis.** `[synthesis]` A random unit-phase surrogate has **no** cubic-coherent term; the
real curve does (P18). Under the fixed L2 budget, energy spent on the coherent cubic concentration must
be **withdrawn from the incoherent mean magnitude** — which is *exactly* a sub-Rayleigh mean deficit.
So **N7 is not an unexplained residue — it is the shadow of P18's coherence against the surrogate.** The
project already suspected "budget"; the new content is naming the *specific* structure (the proved cubic
channel) that occupies the budget, which the generic row-budget statement does not.

**Cheapest decisive test.**
1. Compute the coherent cubic energy fraction `ρ_cubic = |Σ_bχ₂(b)T³|² / (Σ_b|T|²)³`-normalized, and the
   N7 mean-deficit `δ`, on the **same** primes. **Prediction:** `δ ∝ ρ_cubic` across primes.
2. **Scaling discriminator (the firing control):** P18's coherent term scales like `n²`; if N7 is its
   budget-shadow, `δ` must carry the **same `n`-scaling** as `ρ_cubic`. A different scaling refutes α.
3. **Removal test:** project out the `k=3×χ₂` coherent component from `T`, recompute the mean magnitude —
   the deficit should shrink toward the surrogate.

**[caveat]** k=3 is **a₀-generic** (holds even at a=2), while N7 is stated for the DLP harmonic — so α
cannot be that k=3 *causes* an a-specific effect; it is that the **a-generic cubic coherence** sets the
budget floor the surrogate lacks, and N7 measures the real-vs-surrogate gap. The removal test controls
for this directly.

**Builds toward.** If confirmed, N7 dissolves into a **sharper law** (deficit = a computable functional of
the proved cubic term) — the anomaly becomes a quantitative prediction, and the "#1 lead residue" gets a
mechanism. If refuted by the scaling, N7's budget is spent elsewhere (narrows the search to non-cubic
coherent modes). Either outcome is progress. **Scope:** model `p` (enumerable); no secp transfer — but see η.
