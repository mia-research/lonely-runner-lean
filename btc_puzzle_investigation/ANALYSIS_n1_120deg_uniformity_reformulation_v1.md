> `Frob³` has each eigenvalue with multiplicity 2, equivalently the `A_new`-twist L-function

`{α³, α³, −ᾱ³, −ᾱ³}` — the square of `(1−α³T)(1+ᾱ³T)`. Conversely, a perfect square forces two of the

## 2. Why this is the right target for `L-mono` (and not just a restatement)

The per-cell statement was a **ratio** condition (`α₂/α₁=ζ₃`) — a statement about two specific complex
numbers, which no group action within one fiber can force (v3 §2's category error). The reformulation
is a **rank/multiplicity** condition on `H¹_c(F_a)⊗_{F_p}F_{p³}`. Multiplicity-2 spectra are the natural
output of **geometric structure** — a `ℤ/3` symmetry, an induced representation, a square-root local
system. This is precisely the register `L-mono` lives in: *"the sheaf has a `μ₃` in its monodromy
acting on `H¹_c` with the `ζ₃`-ratio as an eigenvalue relation."* The reformulation converts that vague
target into a decidable one: **exhibit the doubling of `Frob³` geometrically.**

## 3. The cubic cover — the natural candidate, and the exact obstruction `[OPEN]`

`j(E)=0`, `ζ₃∈F_p`, so `[ω]:(x,y)↦(ζ₃x,y)` is an order-3 `F_p`-automorphism of `E`. Its quotient is
computable: invariants are generated by `y` (since `x³=y²−7` is already a function of `y`), so

> **`π: E → P¹_y`, `(x,y)↦y`, is a cyclic `ℤ/3`-cover** (Galois group `⟨[ω]⟩`), branched at `y=±√7`
> (`x³=0`, totally ramified) and `y=∞`.

`L_{χ₂(y)} = π^*M` is pulled back from the base (a function of `y`), hence `ℤ/3`-invariant. **The
obstruction is the `a`-twist:** `[ω]^*L_{χ_a} = L_{χ_{λa}} ≠ L_{χ_a}` for `a≠0` (`λ` = order-3 in
`(ℤ/n)^*`). So `F_a` is **not** `ℤ/3`-equivariant, and `π_*F_a` does **not** split into `χ₃`-eigensheaves
on `P¹_y` in the naive way. **This is exactly why `[ω]`/the ω-descent relates the cells `{a,λa,λ²a}`
rather than splitting one fiber (v3 §2), and it is why the mechanism is not immediate.**

**The precise open question `L-mono` must answer:**

> `Frob³`-modules — and if so, what is `X`? The `ℤ/3`-cover `π` and the cell-cycle
> `F_a → F_{λa} → F_{λ²a}` (which `[ω]` realizes as an `F_p`-morphism intertwining `Frob`) are the raw
> material; the `a`-twist is the thing to be absorbed, not ignored.

Candidate to test next: `[ω]` gives an `F_p`-isomorphism `H¹_c(F_a) ≅ H¹_c(F_{λa})` commuting with

## 4. The other lever `L-Stick` — one honest note

For `a=0` the eigenvalues are `√p × μ₁₂` (supersingular Gauss sums, Stickelberger-factorized). For
`a≠0` the angle equidistributes (v2 §2) — the eigenvalues are **not** `√p ×` roots of unity, so they are
