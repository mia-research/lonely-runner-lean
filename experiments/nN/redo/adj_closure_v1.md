# adj_closure_v1 -- adjacency search over fine cells of gate crossers

Directory `experiments/nN/redo/`. All files new, prefix `adj_`, suffix `_v1`. Every stated fact is an exact-Fraction computation (`path()`/`outcome()`/`cell_rows` signatures, exact interval windows, exact dual certificates, strict-inequality checks of every point used). Floats (scipy highs) only chose points, bases and directions. Negatives are "not reached by (search description)".

## 1. Method (what was done)
- **Fine cell** of an arrangement x (readings sorted, rho_1 = 1): the rows of `cell_rows(x)` (closure polytope P, homogeneous linear rows incl. rank-order rows), signature = blake2b-128 of `sigkey(tags, steps)` (path, statuses of every reading at every visited state, setter comparisons, rank order).
- **Gate crosser**: `path(x)` has a setter 0 (reading 1) and landing > 7 (`crosses2`).
- **Facets** of a cell: rows grouped by identical integer vector; for each group a float LP (max margin t with the row = 0 and all other groups >= t) decides whether a facet exists; an exact point p on the facet is built (rationalise, project exactly onto the hyperplane, check every other row strictly positive in Fractions). Crossing: y = p + eps d with d = -(gradient)/|gradient|^2, eps = 2^-m, m increased until (i) the crossed row is negative and all other rows strictly positive (exact), and (ii) the signature at y equals the signature at p + (eps/8) d (stability). y is sorted/normalised, `crosses2(y)` decides gate crosser; a crosser's signature is a (new or known) node; a non-crosser is a **crossing-losing facet crossing**, recorded with its causing row.
- **One row at a time.** No pair of rows was crossed. Two kinds of lower-dimensional handling were used: (a) a cell whose closure has no interior point in the 6-dimensional slice but whose rows contain opposite pairs (a = 0 forced) is expanded inside its affine hull (facets of the hull face, exact, as above) and, for each independent forced equality, crossed off the hull in both signs, one equality at a time (354 such crossings from 177 cells, all 354 stay gate crossers); (b) for every full-dimensional facet, the exact facet point p itself is evaluated: if p is a gate crosser lying in a different cell (a tie stratum), it is counted and its template recorded but **not expanded**.
- Rank-order rows are included in every cell; none of them was a facet in any of the 6,826 expanded cells (0 facets of kind `order`), so no order-row crossing occurred.
- **Start cells** (`adj_reps_v1.py`): one tuple per K6 template of the 16,152 stored crossers (115 templates, reproduced exactly), X24, and the 5 largest-rho_7 crossers (X24 is among them): 121 starts, 118 distinct cells.
- **BFS** (`adj_bfs_v1.py`): FIFO queue, resumable state (`adj_state_*_v1.pkl`), 540-575 s per run, one process at a time. Modes: `unb` (no bound), `box` (row 25 rho_1 - rho_7 >= 0 added to every cell, not crossable), `e344` (single extra start, see 3.6).

## 4. What a closed frontier would and would not have shown (item 5)
A closed frontier from these start cells would show: the set of gate-crossing fine cells connected to the starts by single-facet crossings (as found by the procedure) is finite, with the listed count. It would **not** show: (i) that no other connected component of gate-crossing cells exists (components not containing a start); (ii) that cells joined only through faces of codimension 2 or more are included (no pair of rows was crossed; 354 off-hull single crossings were done for the 177 codimension-1 cells; for rows tangent to a cell at a lower-dimensional face, 95 rows with margin in (-1e-6, 0] in a sample of 120 cells, nothing was crossed); (iii) that no facet escaped the float screen (facet LPs with float margin <= 1e-12 are treated as not facets; in the 120-cell sample `adj_thin_v1.out` the margins of the 29,940 tested rows show no value in (0, 1e-5], so the screen has a gap there, but a cell or facet thinner than that is not excluded); (iv) that the neighbour found is the neighbour across that whole facet (one exact point per facet is crossed; a facet with several adjacent cells along its extent was crossed at one point only); (v) anything about tie strata (lower-dimensional cells reached only as facet points were counted, not expanded); (vi) anything about arrangements with rho_k above the LP box 10^6 or about the unreached 4,531 stored-crosser cells except through the search. Float-chosen points: every point used was verified exactly; the choice of which facet point (one per facet) and which direction was float-chosen.
The fine-cell templates are not cell invariants (96 of 6,826 cells show two templates), so "templates reached" means "templates of the evaluated exact points".

## 5. Exceptions and caveats
1. Not closed; cut by budget (14,325 reached, 7,499 unexpanded).
2. Template statements are about evaluated points: entry point, interior centre, crossing neighbours, facet points (6 of the 8 outside templates come from tie points or codimension-1 cells).
3. 2 of the 115 templates were not met by this search (not reached; not shown absent).
4. rho_k "maxima" are cell limits that no arrangement attains (vertex arrangements are not gate crossers); attained arrangements are given as exact interior points (distance 2^-30 of the way to the vertex), not as maxima.
5. Single-row crossings only. Codimension-2 contacts were not crossed. Off-hull handling covers cells whose forced equalities are opposite row pairs; every codimension-1 cell met had that form (no cell had status other than ok).
6. The LP box for rho_2..rho_7 is [1, 10^6]; facet existence beyond it is not tested.
7. Signature equality is hash equality (blake2b-128) of the exact signature tuple.
8. Facet LP margins: `adj_thin_v1.out` (120 expanded full-dimensional cells, 29,940 rows tested): no row with margin in (0, 1e-5]; 95 rows with margin in (-1e-6, 0]; 840 infeasible; 169 with margin in (1e-5, 1e-3]; 1,308 above 1e-3.
9. The `.out` label `?(...)` in section D2 is a row reduced modulo a lower-dimensional hull (three rows, 83 crossings).
