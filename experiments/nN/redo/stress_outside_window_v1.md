# Stress outside the tree windows (v1)

Everything below is an exact check (gmpy2 mpq cascade in `stress_lib_v1.py`, cross-checked every 40th-50th arrangement against the official Fraction `path()`/`outcome()`: 0 mismatches in every run). Floats only chose annealing acceptance and which LP-free candidates to try. Negatives are "not reached by" the stated search.

Checks applied to every arrangement (`check()` in `stress_lib_v1.py`): (G) landing>7 iff reading 1 sets a lap-1 move iff a state equals 9, and no landing in (7,9); (C) closer is an argmin of (7-p_y)/rho_y, and closer = opener's reading only if the opener is rank 7; (S) prefix-distinct setters give rho_{7-k} > 7(7/9)^k, step ratio <= (8j+1)/(8j-1) <= 9/7, consecutive setters differ; class label against K0..K2z with K2a, K2s1 empty; structural anomalies (negative width, nm=1 opener not its own strip lap, nm>=2 opener zone containing R=1, landing not the opener's exit).

5. Lap-triple family R* = 9(8a+1)(8b+1)/((8c1-1)(8c2-1)) (extremal a=11, b=4, c1=c2=6). In the range a<60, b<25, c1=c2=c in 2..12 with value in (26433/2209, 24) and 1 < R17 < value, 150 triples were sampled (random order). Result: no triple realised: best exact landing over the 150 is 49500/4747 = 10.4276 (triple a=17,b=4,c=6, value 18.42; reading 5 blocked at 9 and a state at R17, opener (2,3), not (7,17), class K2z). The constructive beam (cap 25) did not reach a gate crossing (best landing 6.06 in 7 readings). The search is much weaker than the tree: it did not recover 11.97 in the cap-25 window either, so these negatives say only "not reached by this search".
6. Stopping constraints that were actually observed (exact, from b12 and from the walks):
   - The extremal chain's bound uses exactly two rows: reading 5 blocked at the gate state 9 at lap c1 (9 rho_5 > 8c1-1) and reading 7 blocked at R17 at lap c2 (R17 rho_7 > 8c2-1), with R17 = (8b+1)/rho_5. Both integers (8c-1) enter the bound with their blocking laps; the bound is R* <= 9(8a+1)(8b+1)/((8c1-1)(8c2-1)) on the cell. Smaller c gives a larger bound but the path then has to supply the intermediate blocking states; in the 150 constructed triples the gate state 9 was not reached by the path (149 of 150), so the family stops at the gate, not at the inequality.
   - Gate crossing with a large reading: arithmetic fact (exact): a state 9 needs at least 9 moves from R=1, since each move has ratio <= (8j+1)/(8j-1) <= 9/7 and (9/7)^8 = 7.467 < 9 < (9/7)^9 = 9.60; a move of a reading with rho in zone j has step <= 2R/(8j-1). In the walks every reading of the gate-crossers was active; the reached maxima were rho_7 = 16.21 and rho_6 = 8.14. No ceiling is derived; these are the values the walks reached.
