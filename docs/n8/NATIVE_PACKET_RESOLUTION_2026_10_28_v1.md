# Native packet compression and opening reversals — 2026-10-28, v1

[L] A pure two-reader reversal has a forced local resolution: a reader at its own opening, followed by a captured partner opening, either stays clear there or returns and then clears BOTH readers at its next own opening. Further capture must involve a third physical reader; this is not a global n8 move bound.
[L] Let dB=N+1/8, let s be captured atB and open atC, and let d be recaptured atC with independent return levelm=advance(d,B,C). Thenm≥1 and (4m−1)s<d, hence3s<d. The inequalities come from d(C−B)>m−1/4 and s(C−B)<1/4.
[L] At the following d-openingD, s retains its original clear window because s≤3d; d itself is at its own lower boundary. This rules out an immediate d→s→d→s reversal without introducing an outsider.
[L] partnerEnd(d,s,B) selects C if d is clearC, otherwise the next d-openingD. pair_last_move_and_clear supplies an ACTUAL pair move into that endpoint and proves both readers clear, with no pair-endpoint clearance hypothesis.
[L] PairRecoveryClass derives CanonicalReturnClass when all physical outsiders recover at partnerEnd. PairGuardClass discharges those duties using one earliest outsider closing and initial outsider clearance. Both join the existing catalogue; every physical speed is positive/distinct, the subject keeps moving, and equal absolute disparities are allowed.
[L] pair_endpoint_dichotomy proves isolation at the derived pair endpoint OR a capture of a third physical reader. It does not assert that later outsiders never return or that three-reader exchanges stop.
[L] reversal_interval_endpoint proves a whole real partner-speed interval shares the SAME final anchor opening. Conditions retain partner entry labelM and anchor return labelL using strict endpoint capture inequalities; the intermediate partner opening varies inversely with its speed. No sampling supplies the universal interval proof.
[L] Fragment(ds,q,G,N) allows an arbitrary supplied sequence of positive subgroup moves G_j⊆ds, including independent return labels and differing intermediate maxima. If its endpoint clearsds, fragment_first_union_isolation proves it is the first ds isolation afterq_0.
[L] If a supplied fixed-ds cascade also ends isolated, fragment_fixed_union_endpoint proves its endpoint equals the fragment endpoint. Thus completed recurring fragments compress to their fixed participating union without retaining a common intermediate history. No fixed-union endpoint or fragment exit is asserted to exist.
[L] Fragment captures at a clear-union exit lie outside that union. fragment_recovery_into_canonical joins actual last moves and explicit outsider recovery; recurring proper-union words need no separate proof for each internal exchange word.
