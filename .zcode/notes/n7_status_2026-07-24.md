## Two real mathematical blockers (not engineering)

From `SECTION6_PLAN.md` "Known risks" + open-subtlety notes — these are the
actual hard parts, independent of who/what writes the Lean:

1. **`five_avoid_m1` statement is false as written.** Its own docstring says it
   needs the 6th element `d₆` (level 1: `7∣d₆ ∧ ¬49∣d₆`) + the gcd=1 constraint
   `¬∃ p prime, (∀i, p∣dᵢ) ∧ p∣d₆`, reformulated jointly over ZMod 98 as a single
   `decide` (~968 valid + 3 gcd cases). This ripples up to `five_avoid`'s call
   site (line 2932) and to wherever `d₆` is available (`bs_discrete_q` dispatch).
   **Cross-cutting refactor, not an isolated sorry-fill.**

2. **`decide` over ZMod 49/98 can silently fall back to `sorryAx`** (the plan's
   own warning). A `lake build` "success" with heavy `decide` may be fake-green.
   Rule: always `#print axioms` after a heavy `decide`; prefer explicit-element
   forms over arbitrary-Finset forms.

Plus the open BS-reading subtleties (Lemma 9(ii) mixed-pair carry, Lemma 9(i)
ν=m edge) flagged "read BS carefully, do NOT assume."

## Realistic assessment: autonomous local-LLM proof production

**No.** There is no target in the current n=7 tree where a 4B local model
(qwen3.5:4b) produces correct Lean autonomously with light oversight:

- The bounded-decidable target (`five_avoid_m1`) is blocked behind a
  statement-level refactor (blocker 1) and carries a fake-green risk (blocker 2).
- The unblocked targets (`case_61/65/66`) are research-grade proof construction
  — their proven neighbour `case_62a` is ~150 lines of intricate case-weave
  (`lemma10` numbering → window-closers → antipodal doubling → `five_lambda0`).
  `case_61` is the harder \|A₁\|=5 version. This is at the edge of what frontier
  models do well, and beyond a 4B model's reliable range.
- The interior gap (#5) is a BS ũ-table sub-case — pure proof work.

Consistent with CLAUDE.md §0 (historical) and §2b: a weak-model subagent "is not
the same work done more carefully"; without decidable-artifact + context
isolation it manufactures results. An autonomous proof loop has neither.

## What local-LLM assist *can* do here (advisory, every output checked)

- **Gloss Lean → prose** for `docs/n7_bs/` (low risk; prose).
- **Draft theorem *statements*** from informal BS text — statements are checkable
  (`lake build` typechecks the statement with no body). Proofs are not.
- **Candidate mathlib lemma names** — always verify via `lean_loogle` / build.
- **Sage enumeration scripts** for the §7 m=1 search (independent of Lean; Python
  runs or it doesn't) — this is the one bounded-decidable task, *once* blocker 1
  is resolved.
- **Bounded Q&A** while the strong model drives: "is BS step X a pigeonhole?"

The division of labor is the reverse of "autonomous Qwen": strong model produces
proofs, Qwen gives occasional bounded advisory input.
