## Gotchas (this mathlib)

- `Finset.card_sdiff` is **unconditional**: `#(s\t) = #s − #(t∩s)` (no subset arg);
  follow with `Finset.inter_univ`.
- `not_mem` → `notMem` (`card_insert_of_notMem`, `notMem_singleton`).
- Bare `univ` = `Set.univ` (file-level `open Set`) — write `Finset.univ` for standalone.
- `field_simp` here fully closes goals — a trailing `ring` errors "no goals".
- `rcases h with rfl` on `i = e` (both vars) substitutes **e**, not i — use
  `rcases h with h | …` then `rw [h]`.
- `linarith` won't relate `2/vF` and `1/vF` — supply `(2/vF = 2*(1/vF))` by `ring`.
- `timeout` is unavailable on this macOS; just run `lake build` directly.
