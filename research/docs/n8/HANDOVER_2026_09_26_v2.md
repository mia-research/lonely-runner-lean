## 1. The owner's rules (stated or restated this session)

1. **Exact arithmetic only.** No floating point in any decision (`Fraction`, `gmpy2.mpq`).
2. **The Boundary Set Method, not brute force.** Zones are open; touching zones do not merge; a cell is decided by an
   interior point.
3. **No runner is ever stationary; every speed is positive.** Write arrangements with positive speeds, e.g.
   `(1, 1 + s₁, …)`, or the subject at `max ds + 1` with runners at `c ± d`. The Lean target admits all reals, which is
   equivalent by `lonelyAt_add_const`. Memory: `feedback_positive_speeds_no_stationary_runner_2026_09_25`.

   `LonelyRunnerAt8` adds no requirement.

   through the cascade and design tests from how the isolation occurs; do not search when the cascade can show the
   mechanism.
8. **Lower orders 3–7 are native.**
9. **"Runner 1 is runner 8's neighbour on the circle":** prove it at one end and it is proved everywhere. This is now
   a theorem (§3).

## 2. The framework, as understood now

**The picture (owner's, and the target).** The φ(n) law gives the arithmetic progression's global isolation. Every
arrangement is a deformation of a progression. The claim is that a deformation *relocates* each runner's isolation point
and never destroys it; the cascade shows where it moves.
- **Observed only on traced paths:** E53 (one runner, 39 steps, every `R ≤ 475/99`) and E54 (two runners, 5,408
  subject-events, none lost, `R ≤ 17/4`) `[VERIFIED — exact traces]`.
- **As a theorem** it is `DeformationN.lonely_along_path(_of_margin)`, with two explicit hypotheses:
  - local stability: a margin where the lonely set has interior, and the **two-pinner lemma** at isolated instants,
    which is owed in general;
  - a fixed window of instants `[c, T]`: the owner's "bounded by the speeds", supplied by the box certificates only
    where `d_max ≤ 24·d_min`, and owed in general.

**Coordinates.** `R = 8·d_min·t`; `R = 1` is the first spine instant `1/(8·d_min)`.
- The **event's** `R` never lies in `(7, 9)` (Thm 11.2); cascade steps can.
- A move multiplies `R` by less than `9/7` (Thm 6.25).
- Band lemma: in a band the cascade reaches its event or leaves through the next gap, so "no climb leaves band `b`"
  is existence `[PROVED — in the text, E46; not in Lean]`.

**`d_min` sets the scale** (E53) `[VERIFIED — exact traces]`.
- While `d_max ≤ 7·d_min`, the event is `1/(8·d_min)`, sliding with `d_min` and pinned by the circle neighbour.
- When `S` crosses 7 it jumps to another AP instant (e.g. `3/8`) or a band-0 instant.
- Grids re-form the global event.

**Late events** (past the first lap):
- Every late climb passes through `(7, 9)`, and there the nearest runner sets the move to `R = 9`
  `[PROVED — Thms 11.2, 6.25; VERIFIED 37 of 37]`.
- Some runner is in the subject's slot at `R = 1`. E41's "the far runner starts it" holds on its 37 cases only.
- For the 37 late events with `S < 9` `[EMPIRICAL]`, readings are near `1, 2.3–2.5, 3.2–3.3, 4.1–4.3, 5.1–5.4, 5.6–6.0,
  7.0–7.8`, and extra laps on the far runner end all 37 `[VERIFIED — 37 of 37]`. That is evidence against late events
  near 16 or 24, **not a proof**. The `S ≈ 9.26` witnesses (E47: about `1, 2.97, 4.03, 5.04, 6.46, 7.19, 9.26`) do not
  follow the template.
- Late events exist up to `S = 2993/273 ≈ 10.96` `[VERIFIED — exact boxes, E47/E48]`.
- **They are knife-edge** `[EMPIRICAL]`: 0 of 2,289 random runners with `S ∈ (7, 8)` are late, and shifting any single
  speed by 2–5% of `d_min` removes lateness.
- All 52 late events on file have a close pair **not** including the subject: min gap ratio `264/107 ≈ 2.467`
  (`late_gap_ratio_v1.out`) `[EMPIRICAL]`.

**Gaps and shapes** (owner). For the slowest runner the readings are running sums of the speed gaps, with
`d_min = g₁` (its own gap) and `d_max = W` (the spread). The shapes are even, clumped with outliers, and even with a
clump; giant spacing and all-clumped fall in the even class by scale.

**Blocked stretches and scale** (E57–E59, E64–E66):
- `L·min(min F, σ₅) ≤ 13/4` for a six-runner block, where `σ₅` is the tightest five-spread (found by local ascent from
  51 starts; not proved). The start-up stretch is harmless: `Q₀ ≤ 0.196` `[EMPIRICAL]`.
- A plain count cannot give the window lemma: `6 × 1/4 > 1`.
- The extremal block `[165, 166, 337, 494, 505, 772]` has its stretch centred at `t = 1/2`: odd and even readings sit
  half a lap apart (a resonance at `p/q`).
- A stretch bridging `k` clear bands of the slowest runner has `L·d₁ < k + 5/4`.
- If the block law holds, Case C closes wherever `min(d₂, σ₅) ≥ (13/3)·g₁` (E58). The exceptions are a larger core
  and a tight five-group.
- Link slack drifts linearly per band `[PROVED — CoreDriftN]` at `η = (nε′ − n′ε)/(n + ε)`, the deviation from the
  progression at the block's smallest gap.
- The relay word does **not** repeat band to band: far runners substitute. In the top blocks every such mover is near
  `p/q` with `q ≤ 4` `[VERIFIED — exact]`, i.e. near-progression at the finer gap `d₁/q`.
