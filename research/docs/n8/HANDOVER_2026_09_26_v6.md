## 0. Read this first **[v5]**: what a fresh session gets wrong here

A new or compacted session drifts back to its training: proofs shaped like the literature, termination arguments,
difference sets, "hard cases". Every item below happened in this work, was corrected by the owner or by a check, and cost
time. Read them before proposing anything.

**The frame.**
1. **No runner is ever stationary, and a difference set is not the object.** Evaluating one runner's difference set with
   the subject at 0 voided a whole stretch of work (the "divisible by 7" artefact, E21 of the first series). The object is
   the arrangement of eight positive speeds; every runner is evaluated in its own readings `|v_j − v_k|`; AP, grid and
   mod 8 are read on the actual speeds. Reading one runner's readings is fine *as a lemma about that runner*.
2. **The cascade is not a search, not an algorithm, not a black box, and does not have to terminate.** It identifies
   where the isolation is. I repeatedly reframed the proof as "why the cascade must stop" (termination, move budgets,
   the grand-return bound as a search limit). The owner rejected each: the event exists by the deformation of the AP's
   global isolation; what is owed is the mechanism. The cascade is the oracle: when a claim fails, perturb the case
   through the cascade and read the mechanism (E102, first series).
3. **The flank is a consequence of isolation, not its cause** (E37b). Choosing a flank first and checking the rest is a
   search. At the event some runner sits exactly one eighth away; that runner is the flank after the fact. In Lean the
   cascade-directed door is `DoorArrives` (`∃ N`, no cap); the obligation of record is still `DoorFlank` (owner's call).
4. **What a proof must be** (`Spec/Target.lean`): a term of `LonelyRunnerAt8`, either by existence or by exhibit (a
   written-down witness map, or a finite case split with each case's instant named). "The least instant that works" is
   not a witness. No cap, no bound on the lap, no first-lap requirement: `DoorFirstLap` is refuted, and there is no
   one-lap requirement anywhere (I once implied one; the owner caught it).
5. **Lower orders 3–7 are native framework results** (v21 §17, owner ruling 2026-09-25), not "tools used by
   permission". Using order seven inside an order-eight proof (e.g. `TrainCollapseN`, merge two readings, six left) is an
   eight-runner proof. I confused this twice: first calling the 1/7 a stationary-runner slip, then over-correcting.
