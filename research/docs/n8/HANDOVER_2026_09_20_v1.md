# Handover: the true state of Existence at order 8

Written to close out this arc accurately, so the next session does not repeat its mistakes.

## 1. Is Existence proved in Lean?

**No.** `exact?` cannot close `LRC.Spec.LonelyRunnerAt8`. Every theorem concluding it takes a
hypothesis: `lonelyRunnerAt8_of_existence`, `_of_doorFlank`, `_of_doorArrives`, `_of_doorCapped`,
`_of_doorFAM`, and `RealDoor.lonelyRunnerAt8_of_window`. None of those hypotheses is discharged.

`Spec/` and `Cascade/` contain **zero `sorry`** — because the target is a `Prop`, not a hole. That
makes a complete scaffold look like a complete proof. It is not one.

**LRC at n = 8 is open in the literature.** `intLRC_seven` (Barajas–Serra 2008) is the frontier.
The census attack in `Cascade/` closes ~4,400 seven-difference families by `decide`; a complete
census would be the proof, and it is not complete.
