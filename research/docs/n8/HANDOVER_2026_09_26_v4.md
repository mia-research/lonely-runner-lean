## 1. The owner's rules

1. **Exact arithmetic only** (`Fraction`, `gmpy2.mpq`); no floating point in any decision.
2. **Boundary Set Method:** open zones, closed clear set; touching zones do not merge; a cell is decided by an interior
   point.
3. **No runner is ever stationary; every speed is positive.** Never describe a reading set through a runner at rest,
   not even to explain the literature form. An integer set of `n − 1` numbers is the slowest runner's **readings**:
   write the subject at `c > 0` and the others at `c + r` (`{1…7}` → speeds `8…15`). Memory
   `feedback_positive_speeds_no_stationary_runner_2026_09_25`. The Lean target admits all reals, which is equivalent by
   `lonelyAt_add_const`; the `Target.lean` rest-frame docstring was fixed in `8e2bd78`.
4. **Pre-registration is ECC work only.** `CLAUDE.md` §2 and `experiment-designer` were scoped to
   `btc_puzzle_investigation/` and `secp256k1_arithmetic/` (commit `1c4daa1`).
5. **Density is of no use; the framework works natively on all positive reals** (E9/E37). Counts over arrangements
   say nothing about why any one stops; the object is the exact per-arrangement mechanism. **Owner ruling
   2026-09-26 (chat, logged E101):** a measure argument inside a single window *"can be provisionally used if it
   completes the proof part"*; none has been used. **Pigeonhole is a tool, not an object** (E37). Covering is
   admissible.
6. **The conjecture asks only for a lonely time.** A witness is not required; a proof by contradiction counts;
   `LonelyRunnerAt8` adds no requirement.
7. **The cascade is not a search.** It goes straight to the isolation; use it to see the mechanism. When an explanation
   and the cascade disagree, the explanation is wrong.
8. **Reason; do not reach for the shape of an earlier proof.** Hand-offs, pools, exhaustion and termination counting
   were imports the owner rejected. "Hard cases" is a misnomer: the cascade resolves every arrangement instantly.
9. **Do not dismiss a derivation as "too strong".** Check its hypotheses; it may generalise to all `n` (E60).
10. **AP and grid are different tools.** The AP is scale invariant (isolated at `a/(8c)` for any `c`); the grid is set by
    residues mod 8 (reduced speeds a complete residue system, e.g. speeds `1…7, 16`). Mixed and matched, they provide
    for any arrangement. Owner: *"any speed is just AP/grid and a deviation that is forced by d_min"*; runners need not
    agree on a scale (E86); *"reverse patterns show up all the time"*, as the AP's four global instants are two
    patterns and their inverts (E86); *"place most of the front half in AP and grid can take care of the rest"* (E76);
    a relocation is *"not a jump, more displaced"* (E69); *"there is no arrangement that can move so it isn't isolated"*
    (E71); runners are bounded by their neighbours, and *"crowd them too evenly and you have AP again"* (E92).
11. Lower orders 3–7 are native; one end suffices (the slowest runner).
12. Long runs under `caffeinate`, with checkpoints, a progress file, early positives and a positive control for every
    zero. **Never run `multiprocessing` from inline code (stdin) on macOS**: the workers cannot start and the parent
    respawns them (a 24-minute runaway, E79); use a script file.
13. Documents: one append-only running log; new versions, never overwrites; no manuscript or paper version without the
    owner's OK.

## 2. The framework, as understood now

**Coordinates.** `R = 8·d_min·t`; `R = 1` is the bound `1/(8·d_min)`, the first spine instant. The event's `R` never lies
in `(7, 9)` (Thm 11.2); a move multiplies `R` by less than `9/7` (Thm 6.25). Band lemma: in a band the cascade reaches
its event or leaves through the next gap `[PROVED — in the text, E46; not in Lean]`.

**The geometry.** For readings `d = (d₁ < … < d₇)`, time traces the ray `t·d`; only its direction matters (scale
invariance), and mod 8 makes the clear places a grid of boxes (each coordinate of `8t·d` in `[8m + 1, 8m + 7]`).
Isolation is the ray entering a box. For whole-number readings every isolation is a rational instant `a/N` with every
residue `a·d mod N` in `[N/8, 7N/8]`; the cascade uses `N = 8e`, `a = 8k + 1` (`clear_at_int_flank_iff_emod`). Why every
positive ray enters a box is the open question.

**The views** (each an AP/grid reading at one instant):
- **B, the bound** `1/(8·d_min)`: the AP at the nearest runner's scale. R1 (`max ≤ 7·min`) is sufficient; the exact
  condition is the grid at the bound, every residue mod `8·d_min` in `[d_min, 7·d_min]` (E89). `[PROVED]` (R1).
- **L, the ladder** `1/q`, `q ∈ 2…8`: if `q` divides no reading, isolated (memory `project_n8_tight_instances`).
- **N, the grid nudge** `[PROVED — GridNudgeN]`: at an instant where every reading sits on an eighth and the **largest**
  reading is the **only** one on `0 mod 8`, `±1/(8·d₀)` isolates when every other reading is on residues `1…6`
  (forward) or `2…7` (backward). Both fail exactly when residues 1 and 7 are both occupied: the pinned pair.
- **R_q, rung R1** `[PROVED — RungR1N]`: at `a/q ± 1/(8m)`, with the multiples of `q` on whole laps and all in
  `[m, 7m]`, they sit at their own bound; every other reading, at `K + ρ` with `ρ ∈ [1/8, 7/8]`, stays clear if
  `ρ + w/(8m) ≤ 7/8` (forward; `ρ − w/(8m) ≥ 1/8` backward). Half lap: evens in `[m, 7m]`, odds `≤ 3m` ⇒ isolated at
  `1/2 + 1/(8m)`. The half lap is halving (E88); memory's Theorem P (halving) is `[PROVED — in the text, from
  LRC(4)–LRC(7)]` and settles about a tenth of the residual case by counting.
- **V, reverse R1** (the largest reading `e` at `9/(8e)`, others in `[e/9, 7e/9]`): elementary, **owed** as a Lean lemma.
- Also proved: **flank sum** (`FlankSumN`: every runner outside the movers has a whole ratio `A` with `8 ∤ A`, and
  `Σ (1/4 + 1/c) < 1` over the rational movers ⇒ a lonely opening; seats at most three movers, and four or more, which
  reach the sum 1, are the open core of that route); **off-lattice** (`OffLatticeN`; it leaves the generic case, every six
  coprime, 2,811 of 3,000); **split chain** (`SplitChainN`).

**The first move and the chain** (E92–E95, E98–E99):
- **Bound failures are grid collisions,** a reading within `d_min` of a multiple of `8·d_min`. **As operationalised in

- **The first move is a nudge at the nearest runner's scale:** every reading moves forward by `(λ − 1)·d/(8·d_min)`,
  with `λ − 1 < 1/4` (readings `≤ 24`). It fails by an edge push (a reading near `7/8`), a wrap (a far reading carried
  round) or a collider returning. "Only edge pushes" (E93) is forced by the range `≤ 24` (E94: wide sample 23,807 edge,

- **The budget** `[PROVED — Thm 6.25]`: a chain with every setter different has at most 7 moves and ends in the first
  lap (`(9/7)⁷ < 7`). Only repeats go further.
- **Repeaters are the fast colliders** (`ρ` near 8, 16, 21), setting on consecutive laps. A repeat is a timing
  coincidence about one speed unit wide (E98). Each increment is bounded by the repeater's clear band (`6/ρ` in `R`)

  1.446 up to 24, above `6/ρ`.
- **As operationalised in E95** (`|d − s|·t < 1/8` at the repeat), repeats are not slot-sharing: 540 of 9,091. The
  nudges do not shrink step by step.
