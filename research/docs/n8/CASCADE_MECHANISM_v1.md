## 1. The conversion in one statement [P, definitions]
A point R is *clear* for reading y when R rho_y mod 8 is in [1,7] (closed; period 8/rho_y). The clear set of all seven readings is the intersection of seven periodic sets. The landing R* is the least point of that intersection at or above 1. The cascade is an algorithm for finding it: from a blocked state it jumps to the largest exit among the zones containing it; every point passed over lies inside a used zone, so the first unblocked point reached is the least one. The path (rank, lap) of each move is the record of how the jump chain was assembled.

## 2. Carving: how the intersection is built [P]
Reading 1 is clear exactly on [8m+1, 8m+7] (its windows). Within the first window [1,7] carve with readings 2, 3, ..., 7 in turn: K_1 = [1,7], K_k = K_{k-1} minus the zones of reading k. Every window of K_k (a connected piece, one point allowed) is met only by zones of readings above k, so it is crossed only by readings k+1..7 (the chain through it uses no other zones). The final set K_7 is the set of lonely windows in [1,7] and does not depend on the order of the readings.
**One window per zone (L2) [P, T 0/75,998 containments]:** a zone of reading k holds at most one window of K_{k-1}: the gap between two windows inside one zone would be covered by zones of slower readings lying inside the gap, which are wider than the zone. This is the only place the order of the speeds enters the carving.

## 3. The two cases and the two phases [P for (a)-(c); OBS for (d)]
- **K_7 non-empty (window A):** R* is the left end of the first window of K_7. The cascade lands there with at most the moves needed to reach it. No move at all means R = 1 is clear for readings 2..7 (no reading in a strip (8j-1, 8j+1)) [P, T 0/20,000].
- **K_7 empty (the d_min gate, window B):** every point of [1,7] is blocked by some reading from 2 to 7, the chain reaches a state in (7,9) (zones of readings above 1 have width below 2, so a jump from a state at most 7 lands below 9), and R* is at least 9 [P]. That the move from the first state above 7 lands exactly at 9 (reading 1's zone is the setter) is observed in every region of the complete tree up to rho_7 = 25 (7,946 of 7,947; the other is a landing at exactly 7) [TREE, OBS]. From 9 the cascade carves the second window [9,15] by readings 2..7 in the same way, with each reading's phase rotated by 8 frac(rho_y) relative to the first window (p_y(R+8) = p_y(R) + 8 frac(rho_y)) [P]; its landing is the left end of the first window of that carving. Observed: R* < 12 always (maximum 11.966; the first window of the second carving contains or precedes 12).
- **The windows of K_6 in a crossing arrangement (L3) [P, T 12,078/12,078]:** each lies in exactly one zone of reading 7, distinct windows in distinct zones, and each such zone is a move of the path before the gate. So a crosser's path alternates: readings 2..6 cross the stretches between windows, reading 7 crosses each window; the number of reading-7 moves before the gate is at least the number of windows of K_6 (2 to 6 over the templates seen) and rho_7 < 2/(longest window).

## 4. The landing's two ends and the moves [P]
The first lonely window [R*, R* + w] is bounded on the left by the exit of the OPENER (the zone of reading s, lap j, with R* = (8j+1)/rho_s) and on the right by the entry of the CLOSER, the reading minimising (7 - p_y)/rho_y with p_y = R* rho_y mod 8 its position at R* (p_s = 1). The opener's own reading is the closer only if s = 7 (L4) [P, T 1,593/1,593]; hence K2s1 (two or more moves, opener on lap 1, closer = the opener's reading) is empty [P]. Moves obey: setter = largest exit among the blockers [definition, T 0/152,170]; each move multiplies the state by at most (8l+1)/(8l-1) <= 9/7; the setter of the move from R_k has rho > 7/R_k; consecutive setters differ; if the first k+1 setters are distinct then rho_{7-k} > 7(7/9)^k (L5) [P]; a slower next setter links at the same or a lower lap, a faster one at a strictly higher lap (L6) [L, T 0/136,199].

## 5. How the arrangements are categorised by the conversion
1. **No move** (R = 1 clear): window A, K_7's first window starts at 1.
2. **Window A with moves** (landing in [1,7]): the outcome is the first window of K_7; the category is (opener rank, opener lap), closer, width. The opener rank runs over 2..7 (rank 1 cannot open below the gate). These outcomes multiply with the window (opener laps up to 20 for rank 7 at rho_7 <= 25): a fast reading supplies a new landing at each of its laps [TREE].
3. **Window B** (gate crossed): K_6 has 2 to 6 windows, each inside its own reading-7 zone; the chain then passes the gate and carves the second window. These outcomes form a family that is already almost fixed from rho_7 <= 9: 7,855 / 7,940 / 7,947 regions at rho_7 <= 9 / 17 / 25 [TREE], with the opener's lap confined per rank (rank 2: 3-5, 3: 4-6, 4: 5-6, 5: 5-8, 6: 7-9, 7: 8-18 at rho_7 <= 25). Opener 1 occurs only here, at lap 1, landing exactly 9 (640 regions); the other six openers 2..7 occur here too.
4. The 14 classes (no move; openers 2..7 below the gate; openers 1..7 past it) are every combination the conversion allows; they are not a finding by themselves.
**How the chain is carried past the gate [TREE, E377]:** in all 7,947 crossing regions the order in which readings 2 and 3 move is a ladder of alternations (3.1, 2.1, 3.2, 2.2, 3.3, 2.3, ...; one lap higher each round, the link rule of L6): 19 rank sequences, 28 chains with laps, constant over rho_7 <= 9, 17, 25; with the top four readings dropped the crossing chains are 28 at every window; below the gate the chains keep growing with the window (scale of sub-clusters, not yet quotiented).
**The crossing family's alphabet [TREE, E376]:** the crossing regions use 47 / 51 / 52 distinct zones (rank, lap) and 252 / 281 / 294 distinct links between consecutive zones at rho_7 <= 9 / 17 / 25; nothing seen at rho_7 <= 9 is lost later; the new links at 25 all run through reading 7 at laps 10-18.
**A crossing region is lost only at a touch [P by L7 in `GATE_LEMMAS_v1.md`; observed 28,511/28,511]:** every one of 28,511 observed crossing-losing facet crossings was a row "exit of the chain's state = entry of a blocked reading's zone" (an overlap of consecutive zones closing to a point) [OBS]. In the cascade's terms: crossing needs every K_6 window to sit inside a reading-7 zone and every chain overlap to stay positive.

## 6. What the conversion does not yet explain (the proof targets)
- A floor on the longest window of K_6 (equivalently: why the six slower readings always leave a stretch of positive length in d_min's first window, bounded below on a bounded box of the middle readings): the first-window form 2/(7 rho_6) is false (E359). Proving a floor, with a bound on the middle readings of crossers, gives a ceiling for rho_7 and the confinement of the B opener laps (lap j = (rho_s R* - 1)/8).
- R* < 12: the second carving's first window.
- Whether the A outcomes reduce to finitely many once the lap of the fast reading is quotiented out.
