# Audit — evidence base of every standing claim, and the `k ≥ 6` map

2026-09-12. n = 8, individual isolation. Completes the grid-distance map and audits the
mapping series for claims resting on thin evidence.

## 1. New: `k = 6` mapped, `k = 7` impossible

**`[PROVED]` `k ≤ 6`.** `k = 7` means all eight speeds share one residue mod 8, so every
difference is `≡ 0 mod 8` and `8 | gcd` — contradicting primitivity after reduction. Verified
in construction: 0 instances found.

**`k = 6` `[VERIFIED — 1,320 distinct primitive configurations, constructed]`**

| | |
|---|---|
| `max S` | `{4: 320, 6: 1000}` |
| min count | 8 to 26 — **never below 8** |
| spine holders | 0 in every one |

With only two residue classes, no runner has a unique residue, so nobody holds the spine — yet
the minimum count is **8**, four times the floor of the danger band.

## 2. The complete grid-distance map

| `k` | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `max S` | 8 | 6 | 4–6 | 3–6 | 4, 6 | 4–6 | 4, 6 | — |
| **min count** | 4 | **2** | **2** | **2** | **2** | 4 | **8** | impossible |
| spine holders | 8 | 6 | 4 | 2 | 0–2 | 0 | 0 | — |

The danger band is `k = 1..4`. Both ends are safe and for different reasons: `k = 0` by the
spine (proved), `k ≥ 5` by the spread that high `k` forces (slot-counting argument,
`AWAY_FROM_GRID_v1` §2).

`max S` is **not monotone** in `k` — 6 at `k = 1`, 4 at much of `k = 2..4`, back to 6 at
`k = 5, 6`.

## 4. Retest 1 — the collision claim, at 21× scale

"Sharing a residue with two others floors at 4" rested on 5,988 instances. Extended to
`[0..20]`:

| shares residue with | instances | min count |
|---|---|---|
| 0 others | 779,506 | 4 |
| 1 other | 718,844 | **2** |
| 2 others | 127,890 | 4 |

Holds, with the two-collision class now at **127,890** instances. The counterintuitive half of
the result survives the scale-up.

## 5. What should be tested at larger scale or different scale

**Needs more data:**
- `k = 1 ⟹ max S = 6`. Asserted as determinism from one small box. Retest in progress across
  `[0..16]`, `[0..18]` and random `[0..59]`.
- The popcorn structure of the measure. Currently ~30 exact points; the *shape* of the claim
  (jump at every rational, size falling with denominator) deserves a systematic sweep over
  denominators with the jump magnitude recorded.
- Window trajectories. One path only. The fixed-endpoint mechanism should be checked on paths
  where the varying constraint is not the largest, and where more than one constraint moves.

**Needs different scale:**
- Every count result is over *reduced integer* configurations of modest size. The popcorn
  result (`AP_GRID_SUMMARY_v1` §2) shows the measure is generic at irrational speeds, but no
  count-based result has an analogue there, because count-per-cycle is undefined. A
  measure-based restatement of the floor would be scale-free; none exists.
- `k = 6` was reached by construction, not enumeration, because the required spread puts it
  out of reach of any feasible box. Constructed families can carry their own bias; a second
  construction with a different generator would be worth running.

**Does not need retesting:** everything in the `[PROVED]` rows of §3, and the span-38
enumeration, which is exhaustive over 12.5M sets and was independently screened.
