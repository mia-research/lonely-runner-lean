## 2. The framework, as understood now

**The geometry.** For a subject with readings `d = (d₁ < … < d₇)`, time traces the ray `t·d`. Scale invariance means
only the ray's direction matters; mod 8 makes the clear places a grid of boxes (each coordinate of `8t·d` in
`[8m + 1, 8m + 7]`). Isolation is the ray entering a box. For whole-number readings every isolation is a rational
instant `a/N` with every residue `a·d mod N` in `[N/8, 7N/8]`; the cascade always uses `N = 8e`, `a = 8k + 1`
(`clear_at_int_flank_iff_emod`). Why every positive ray enters a box is the open question.

**The views** (each an AP/grid reading of the arrangement at one instant, each with a proved room condition):
- **B, the bound** `1/(8·d_min)`: the AP at the nearest runner's scale. R1 (`max ≤ 7·min`) is sufficient; the exact
  condition is the grid at the bound, every residue mod `8·d_min` in `[d_min, 7·d_min]` (E89).
- **L, the ladder** `1/q`, `q ∈ 2…8`: if `q` divides no reading, isolated (memory `project_n8_tight_instances`).
- **N, the grid nudge** (`GridNudgeN`): at an instant where every reading sits on an eighth and only the largest is on
  `0 mod 8`, `±1/(8·d₀)` isolates unless residue 7 (forward) or 1 (backward) is occupied: the pinned pair.
- **R_q, rung R1** (`RungR1N`): at `a/q ± 1/(8·min D_q)` the multiples of `q` sit at their own bound and the rest ride
  from `r/q` with margin `r/q − 1/8`. The half lap `q = 2` is halving (E88).
- **V, reverse R1:** the largest reading at its second opening `9/(8e)` when the others lie in `[e/9, 7e/9]`.
- Also proved: flank sum (`FlankSumN`, up to three movers), off-lattice (`OffLatticeN`), split chain (`SplitChainN`).

**Where isolation lands** (E100, exhaustive to 24): the cascade's instant is exactly a view in 310,889 of 346,104:
the bound 200,736; one reading's next opening `9/(8e)` 63,806; rung R1 37,438; reverse R1 7,847; ladder 1,062. The
other 35,215 are at a reading's later opening (lap `≥ 2`). The isolation is displaced from the bound to the first
runner-scale with room, in time order.

**What sets how far it is displaced** (E96–E97): the spread `S = d_max/d_min` against 8. `S ≤ 7`: at the bound.
`S` about 7–11: a reading near `8·d_min` collides on its first lap and the displacement is largest (to `R ≈ 5.7` for
readings `≤ 24`; late events to `R ≈ 15` for `S ≤ 2993/273`). Larger `S`: the ceiling falls (about 3.5 by 14, under 2 on
the wide sample to 2,000).

**The first move and the chain** (E92–E95, E98–E99):
- Bound failures are **grid collisions** (a reading within `d_min` of a multiple of `8·d_min`), not neighbour crowding
  (32,913 of 145,368 blockers are in a crowded pair). Crowded runners ride together in one clear eighth (the owner's
  "crowd them evenly and it is AP again").
- The first move is a **nudge at the nearest runner's scale**: every reading moves forward by `(λ − 1)·d/(8·d_min)`,
  `λ − 1 < 1/4`. It fails by an edge push (a reading near `7/8`), a wrap (a far reading carried round), or a collider
  returning. Readings `≤ 24` show only edge pushes, but that is forced by the range (E94).
- A chain with all setters different has at most 7 moves and ends in the first lap (Thm 6.25: `λ < 9/7`,
  `(9/7)⁷ < 7`). **Only repeats go further.** Repeaters are the fast colliders (`ρ` near 8, 16, 21), setting on
  consecutive laps; a repeat is a timing coincidence about one speed unit wide (E98); each adds at most the repeater's
  clear band `6/ρ` in `R`, plus the nearest runner's own zone (+2) once (E99). Repeats are not slot-sharing (540 of
  9,091).
- The nudges do not shrink step by step (E95).

## 4. Where I was wrong or surprised (v3 additions; v2's list still stands)

1. **Proxies.** Window lemmas, settings counts, block laws, clump accounting, `Q(F)` and "hard cases" were each a
   statement about stretches of time, not about where the isolation is. The owner stopped each; the productive object is
   the isolation's location, read from the cascade.
2. **E57's block law (`13/4`) was refuted** (E68): a tight triple at `d₂` in a near-progression gave 5.304.
3. **"Every runner sets at most twice" was refuted** (E68): three settings are easy to reach.
4. **The flank-sum criterion is a corner:** it holds at the event in only 347 of 12,000 (E80); six movers is ordinary.
5. **Stationary-runner slip** (2026-09-26): the tight instances were described through a runner at rest; the memory was
   rewritten through memory-drafter and the log corrected (E75 correction).
6. **Range-forced results:** "only edge pushes" at the first move (E93) was forced by readings `≤ 24` (E94).
7. **Crowding is not what blocks the bound** (E92); grid collision is.
8. **Tooling:** an inline (`stdin`) multiprocessing run crashed and respawned for 24 minutes; a Lean guardrail hook
   blocks any command containing both `git` and the word "push" (rename files, e.g. `displace_*`).
9. **Menu gaps are not hard cases:** the ten "exceptions" to E88 were isolated at the bound itself; R1 had been tested
   instead of the bound instant (E89).
