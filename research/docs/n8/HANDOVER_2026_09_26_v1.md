## 2. The framework, as understood now

- **The picture.** The φ(n) law gives the arithmetic progression's global isolation. Every arrangement is a
  deformation of a progression; a deformation *relocates* each runner's isolation point and never destroys it. The
  cascade shows where it moves.
- **Coordinates.** `R = 8·d_min·t` places the event on the subject's own AP scale. `R = 1` is the first spine
  instant `1/(8·d_min)`. `R` never lies in `(7, 9)` (Thm 11.2). A move multiplies `R` by less than `9/7` (Thm 6.25).
  **In a band the cascade reaches its event or leaves through the next gap** (finitely many openings, `R`
  increasing), so "no climb leaves band `b`" is existence.
- **`d_min` sets the scale** (E53). While `d_max ≤ 7·d_min` the event is `t = 1/(8·d_min)`, sliding smoothly with
  `d_min` and pinned by the circle neighbour. When `S` crosses 7 it jumps to another AP instant (e.g. `3/8`) or a
  band-0 instant. When the arrangement is a grid again, the global event comes back.
- **Late events** (past the first lap, `R > 7`; E39–E41, E47).
  - Every late climb passes through `(7, 9)`, and there the nearest runner sets the move to `R = 9`.
  - Some runner is in the subject's slot at `R = 1`, and the climb is a relay through all runners.
  - The readings follow a template near `1, 2.3, 3.2, 4.2, 5.2, 5.9, 7.5`: the AP deformed by fractions of a slot.
  - Extra laps on the far runner kill every late event.
  - Late events exist up to `S = 2993/273 ≈ 10.96` (not only `S < 9`).
- **Gaps and shapes** (owner). For the slowest runner the readings are running sums of the speed gaps, with
  `d_min = g₁` (its own gap) and `d_max = W` (the spread). The owner's shapes are even, clumped with outliers, and
  even with a clump; giant spacing and all-clumped are the even class by scale. **All 52 late events have a close
  pair that does NOT include the subject** (gap ratio ≥ 2.47).
- **Blocked stretches and scale** (E57–E59, E64–E66).
  - A six-runner block's longest blocked stretch `L` obeys `L·min(min F, σ₅) ≤ 13/4` (found, not proved), where
    `σ₅` is the tightest five-spread.
  - The extremal block `[165, 166, 337, 494, 505, 772]` has its stretch centred at `t = 1/2`: runners with even and
    odd readings sit half a lap apart, so close pairs cover each other's clear bands (a resonance at `p/q`).
  - A stretch is a relay bridging whole clear bands of the slowest runner.
  - Link slack drifts linearly per band at `η = (nε′ − n′ε)/(n + ε)`, the deviation from the progression at the
    block's smallest gap. The relay word does NOT repeat band to band: far runners substitute, and every such mover
    is near a fraction `p/q` with `q ≤ 4`, i.e. near-progression at the finer gap `d₁/q`.
- **Case C by the cascade** (E66). From the bound, the other six clear within runner 2's first band in 1,350 of
  1,350. Every runner sets at most once in 1,333; twice in 16 (short waits); seven times in 1, a tight five-group
  (`σ₅/d₂ = 0.035`), the train resonance.

   `Target.lean:437` docstring). It also said M2 was "not in Lean", but it already was (`f32daf4`, `4ceb484`).
   **Run `git log` before claiming something is not done.**
3. **Openness and boundedness objections** to the deformation argument were wrong. Both tested the wrong object:
   the exact instant, and comparisons *across* arrangements. The event relocates and is bounded by the arrangement's
   own speeds.
4. **Grades and cadence:** a result grade was held back until its positive control reported. Keep doing that.
5. **"Coverage" framing** annoyed the owner, rightly: coverage is a diagnostic, not the proof.
6. **"S ≥ 9 ⇒ first lap"** was suggested from an adversarial search (0 of 120). It is **false**: the exact enumeration
   found 14 late cells at `S ≈ 9.26` (thin cells the search missed). Exact enumeration beats search.
7. **Block constant 1.31** from sampling was wrong: the perturbation climb found `13/4`.
8. **The off-lattice argument** was dismissed in thought as "too strong"; checked, it is a correct general-`n` rule.
   Rule 10.

   Always check the analytic condition, not just "found an instant". The parity two-mover rule's 95% was likewise the
   geometry's chance rate.
10. **Vertex statements**: two readings of "own isolation point" failed as general laws (E43, E44). The owner's claim
    is about each runner at its own event. It holds near the AP only (E44); frame-dependent.
11. **Step 2 "same relay word repeats each band"** was false: movers substitute.
12. **Bugs caught by controls:**
